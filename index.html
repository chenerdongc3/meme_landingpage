FAQ

What exactly is Me?

Me is a physical toy. It has a shell, paper path, printer, and button, but it doesn't connect to large language models or output text answers. It responds to your questions with image memes, refuses to answer when you're stuck in a rut, and gives you an "Earth Online" action task.

How is it different from AI hardware or companion robots?

It's not a Chatbot shell, not an emotional companion device. It doesn't give you answers, emotional value, or opinions. It trusts the human brain's agency, returning association and understanding to the person themselves.

What is the "Book of Questions"?

Me stores the questions you've asked in the cloud and periodically generates a "Book of Questions" report. What gets recorded is not "what you did", but "what you're curious about, what puzzles you" — the things you keep asking about may well be your talent.
